Investigation · RH AI · karta-kueue-admission

Karta + Kueue multi-layer admission

RayJob + strict GPU quota: one job admits, the second waits — while Karta maps the compound CRD independently.

Demo folder: examples/karta-kueue-admission

Roadmap questions

Kueue fit + RH AI direction

  • Can Karta work with Kueue?
    Yes. RayJobs labeled kueue.x-k8s.io/queue-name are admitted against a ClusterQueue; Karta maps the CRD independently.
  • How does it fit the RH AI roadmap?
    Aligns with WG WAS / KEP-6012: Karta’s gangScheduling.podGroups is the portable hierarchy admission and placement can share.

Mental model

Three steps, three owners

STEP 1
Admission — Kueue LocalQueue / ClusterQueue with GPU quota
STEP 2
Translation — Karta Head/worker paths + PodGroup hints
STEP 3
Placement — kube-scheduler / KAI Binds admitted pods (pluggable)

Objective

Exceed quota → Workload Pending

Show that exceeding GPU quota leaves a Workload Pending (queued), while the admitted RayJob proceeds through Karta-described structure into placement — without a custom Ray↔Kueue Go bridge.

Operational note

Enable RayJob in Kueue

Install uses scripts/kueue-values.yaml so Kueue’s RayJob framework integration is on. Without it, queue labels alone will not create Workloads.
  • Operators: Karta + KubeRay + Kueue (with Ray integration)
  • Sibling karta-kueue-dra uses DRA logical GPUs instead of nvidia.com/gpu

RH AI roadmap

What to standardize on

Layer split

Admission in Kueue · translation in Karta · placement pluggable. Share PodGroup language via KEP-6012.

Inspired by Karta

Long-term: emit native Kueue Workloads from maps — not only coexist via labels.

Takeaways

What to remember

  1. Yes, Karta works with Kueue — RayJobs queue on GPU quota without a Go bridge.
  2. Admit vs Pending proves Layer 1; Karta still supplies Layer 2 structure.
  3. Gang hints in the map track WG WAS / KEP-6012 CompositePodGroup.
  4. Placement stays pluggable — default-scheduler or KAI after admission.
